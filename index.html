WORKSPACE GUIDE

Get closer to your molecule.

Choose a sample or open your own structure. Everything runs in your browser.

1. Open

Load PDB, mmCIF, SDF, MOL, XYZ, or PQR files up to 10 MB and 75,000 atoms. The first model or SDF record is displayed.

2. Explore

Switch representations and color schemes. Cartoon and ribbon need a protein or nucleic-acid backbone; small molecules use atom and bond styles.

3. Refine

Hide water or residue types. Undo and redo restore display settings. Camera movements are independent of display history.
