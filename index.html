Application Guide

Actions

  • upload_file Upload: Load a new molecule (.pdb, .cif, .mol)
  • zoom_in Zoom: Magnify the molecule.
  • 360 Rotate: Auto-rotate the view.
  • pan_tool Pan: Shift the molecule view.
  • redo Redo: Revert to previous style.
  • play_circle Render: Force a visual refresh.

Styles

  • timeline Cartoon: Ribbons/tubes for secondary structure.
  • adjust Surface: Solid Van der Waals surface.
  • share Sticks: Display bonds as sticks.
  • bubble_chart Ball+Stick: Atoms as spheres, bonds as sticks.
  • scatter_plot Spheres: Space-filling scaled spheres.
  • waves Ribbon: Flat ribbon trace.

Gemini Assistant (RAG)

The integrated Biotech AI is configured directly in the code. It understands the currently uploaded molecule and can explain structural properties, app usage, and biotech concepts seamlessly. Click the Chat icon (chat) in the top right menu to open the assistant!
